About PrepNova

Welcome to PrepNova, an educational platform created to help students prepare for Rajasthan government exams like LDC, Patwari, and other competitive exams.

We provide simple and easy-to-understand notes in subjects like Science, History, Geography, Hindi, English, Computer, and Rajasthan GK.

Our goal is to make exam preparation easier, faster, and more effective for all students.

Our Mission

To provide free and quality study material for all competitive exam aspirants.
